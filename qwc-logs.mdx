Most problems are surfaced in QuBe Sync: every request, answer and error is on the connection's page in the
dashboard. Some happen before the Web Connector ever reaches us, such as a wrong password, a damaged `.qwc`
file, or QuickBooks refusing the connection. For those, the Web Connector's own log is the place to look.

## Read the log

In the Web Connector, click **View Log**. By default the log is heavily truncated, which often leaves out the
line you need.

## Turn on verbose logging

Intuit explains how in [QBWC logging](https://help.developer.intuit.com/s/article/QBWC-Logging). With verbose
logging on, the log records each call the Web Connector makes and the full error text QuickBooks returns.
Turn it on on your own test machine from the start, and ask a customer to turn it on when a problem needs
more detail than the dashboard shows.

## Web Connector error codes

The Web Connector reports its own problems as `QBWC` codes (QBWC1039, QBWC1085...). Intuit keeps the fixes in
[Fix common Web Connector errors in QuickBooks Desktop](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/open-programs/fix-common-web-connector-errors-quickbooks-desktop/L9slolvg0_US_en_US).
See also [Problems on your customer's PC](/error_handling#problems-on-your-customers-pc).
